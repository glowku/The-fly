# Principle of least privilege

## Source
Wikipedia — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Principle_of_least_privilege

## Extrait
In information security and computer science, the principle of least privilege (PoLP), principle of minimal privilege (PoMP), or principle of least authority (PoLA) requires that, in a particular abstraction layer of a computing environment, every module must be able to access only the information and resources that are necessary for its legitimate purpose.
